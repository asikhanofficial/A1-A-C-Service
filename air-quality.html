Indoor Air Quality & Purification

Whole-home filtration, germicidal UV lights, and dedicated dehumidification systems designed to eliminate airborne allergens, biological growth, and dust.

Healthier Living Spaces

Breathe Clean, Fresh, and Purified Air Every Day

The EPA estimates that indoor air can be 2 to 5 times more polluted than outside air. Because modern homes are tightly sealed for energy conservation, microscopic contaminants—such as pet dander, mold spores, volatile organic compounds (VOCs), and dust mites—remain trapped and continuously recirculated throughout your ductwork.

At A1 A/C Service, we treat the root cause of poor indoor air quality. We install hospital-grade media air cleaners (MERV 11–16), germicidal UVC lamp systems over moist evaporator coils to neutralize mold spores, and dedicated whole-home dehumidifiers to keep indoor relative humidity safely below 50% year-round.

Signs & Symptoms

Warning Signs of Poor Indoor Air Quality

If you or your family experience any of these common issues, your HVAC filtration system needs attention.

Persistent Musty "Dirty Sock" Odors

Caused by mold and mildew colonies propagating on wet evaporator coil surfaces or inside unsealed plenum boxes.

Excessive Dust & Vent Grille Build-up

Standard fiberglass 1-inch filters capture less than 15% of particulates, allowing heavy dust to coat duct surfaces and furniture.

Chronic Allergies & Respiratory Discomfort

Pollen, pet dander, and microscopic dust mite feces recirculating through your ductwork trigger morning congestion.

High Indoor Relative Humidity (>55%)

Warm, clammy air creates an ideal breeding ground for dust mites and toxic mold varieties across drywall and furniture.

The Advantage

Health and Comfort Benefits of Whole-Home IAQ

Up to 99% Allergy Particle Reduction

High-efficiency MERV 13 to 16 media filtration captures pollen, dust, and animal dander before it enters living areas.

Biological Mold Eradication

UVC germicidal lamps continuously bathe the evaporator coil in sterilizing light, destroying mold and bacteria DNA on contact.

Superior Dehumidification

Dedicated dehumidifiers maintain optimal 45% to 50% relative humidity even when the A/C thermostat isn't calling for cooling.

Our Process

Our Indoor Air Quality Improvement Sequence

1

IAQ Audit

We test particulate counts, VOC levels, and relative humidity throughout your property's living zones.

2

Duct & Coil Inspection

We inspect internal duct insulation, plenums, and the evaporator coil chamber for bio-growth or air bypass leaks.

3

Custom Solution Design

We recommend tailored hardware matching your static pressure limits: media filter cabinets, UVC lights, or dehumidifiers.

4

Precision Installation

Our technicians seamlessly integrate purification hardware directly into your existing supply and return ductwork.

5

Post-Install Verification

We re-test indoor particulate levels and review filter maintenance intervals with you for lasting fresh air.
