Troubleshooting Indicators

Common Signs Your A/C Needs Professional Repair

If you observe any of the following symptoms, shut off the system at your thermostat and call +12395178873 to prevent permanent compressor failure.

Blowing Warm or Luke-Warm Air

Caused by low refrigerant, stuck reversing valves, or failed outdoor contactors that prevent the compressor from turning on.

Ice on Refrigerant Lines

Restricted return airflow or low refrigerant pressure drops evaporator coil temperature below 32°F, forming thick ice blankets.

Short-Cycling

The unit starts up, runs for 2 to 3 minutes, and shuts down abruptly. Often caused by failing high-pressure safety switches or thermostat faults.

Grinding or Buzzing Noises

Buzzing indicates electrical relay failures or seized motors. Metal grinding indicates failing compressor bearings or loose blower fans.
