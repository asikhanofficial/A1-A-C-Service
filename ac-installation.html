Precision HVAC Engineering

Invest in Long-Term Comfort and Energy Efficiency

Upgrade Indicators

When Should You Replace Your Air Conditioner?

If your existing air conditioner exhibits multiple symptoms listed below, replacement is usually far more economical than recurring patch repairs.

Unit is Over 12–15 Years Old

Older systems operate at low SEER ratings and suffer internal coil corrosion and compressor fatigue.

Spiking Repair Expenses

When repair costs approach 50% of a brand-new unit, replacement delivers warranty coverage and peace of mind.

Uses Phased-Out R-22

R-22 Freon is no longer produced; recharging an old leaking unit is cost-prohibitive and environmentally obsolete.

Persistent Humidity & Hot Spots

Loss of compressor compression leaves rooms sticky and humid despite the thermostat running non-stop.

The Payoff

Benefits of Modern High-Efficiency Cooling

Up to 30–45% Lower Electric Bills

Upgrading from an older 10 SEER unit to a modern 16–18+ SEER2 inverter system slashes cooling power draw drastically.

Manufacturer Warranty Protection

New installations include comprehensive 10-year parts and compressor warranties, shielding you from unforeseen repair expenses.

Whisper-Quiet Operation

Modern outdoor condensers feature swept-wing fan blades and insulated compressor blankets for nearly silent operation.

Our Process

Our Step-by-Step Installation Protocol

1

Load Calculation

We perform Manual J load calculations to match the precise tonnage your building envelope requires.

2

Safe Removal

We recover old refrigerant according to EPA standards and safely haul away your old obsolete equipment.

3

Mechanical Fitting

We install new condenser pads, hurricane strapping, vibration dampeners, and seamless copper line sets.

4

Deep Vacuum Pull

We pressure test lines with dry nitrogen and pull a deep vacuum below 500 microns to remove all moisture.

5

System Commissioning

We verify subcooling, adjust static duct pressures, set the smart thermostat, and provide owner walkthrough.
