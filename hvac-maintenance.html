HVAC Maintenance & Care Plans

Year-round preventative maintenance programs that optimize cooling and heating performance, protect critical system components, and keep energy costs low.

System Care & Longevity

Bi-Annual Preventative Care for Year-Round Peace of Mind

An HVAC system transitions between rigorous cooling cycles during long humid summer months and heating demands during sudden winter cold snaps. This constant thermal expansion and contraction loosens electrical terminals, stresses fan belts and blower bearings, and tests refrigerant piping joints.

At A1 A/C Service, our comprehensive HVAC maintenance agreements are structured around two seasonal checkouts: a Spring Cooling optimization and an Autumn Heating & Heat Pump inspection. Our certified specialists inspect mechanical safeties, clean combustion and electrical chambers, calibrate smart thermostats, and balance supply airflow throughout your building.

Risk Mitigation

Costly Problems Our Maintenance Plans Prevent

Proactive care stops silent component wear before it leads to full system shutdowns.

Auxiliary Heat Element Burnout

Electric heat strips can short-circuit or trip thermal limit switches when caked with dust and pet dander.

Reversing Valve Seizure

Heat pump valves that switch between heating and cooling can jam if acid accumulates in the refrigeration oil.

Premature Blower Motor Burn

Unbalanced blower wheels and dirty evaporator coils create excessive static pressure that overheats ECM motors.

Efficiency Degradation

Without annual tuning, central systems lose up to 5% of their rated operating efficiency for every year of neglect.

Our Process

Bi-Annual Maintenance Inspection Protocol

1

Controls Audit

Verify thermostat signal calibration, emergency heat sequencing, and outdoor ambient temperature sensors.

2

Mechanical Care

Lubricate motor bearings, inspect fan belts, check blower wheel balance, and tighten mechanical mountings.

3

Drainage Flush

Treat primary and auxiliary drain lines with anti-algae solution and clear condensate traps.

4

Safety Check

Test high-limit temperature cutoffs, float switches, smoke detectors, and carbon monoxide safety margins.

5

Written Report

Deliver a clear digital checklist detailing electrical amp draws, refrigerant charge, and component ratings.
